Patch Notes

v1.11 — Formulas, Hotkeys & Save Hardening

  • New Gem Formulas tab: all 13 base special gem recipes in one table, sorted from easiest to hardest to assemble, with ingredient icons and a one-line effect summary
  • Desktop hotkeys: S toggle speed, R remove, K keep, C combine, L buy life, Esc close modal — button labels show the shortcut in parentheses
  • Save-scum protection: loading a slot now wipes it (delete-on-load), and Save is locked for 5 survived waves after each save
  • Save/Load button greys out with a wave countdown while the cooldown is active; modal cannot be opened at all during cooldown
  • Egyptian Gold (and Gold): a weaker tower's armor debuff can no longer overwrite a stronger debuff still ticking on the target
  • Poison: same-strength hits from the same gem now refresh the duration instead of being ignored mid-tick (applies to Emerald chain and Lucky Jade)
  • Splash % tooltips uncapped — Ruby, Yellow Sapphire chain and Ancient Blood Stone now display the true post-scaling value above 100%
  • Sprite visuals restored for Malachite tiers 2–3 (Vivid / Mighty), the full Dark Emerald chain, Red Crystal tiers 1–2, and the full Star Ruby chain — no more fallback circles on those gems

v1.10 — Late-game Scaling

  • Ground HP growth accelerates past wave 100: +2% of base HP per wave added each 100-wave band (W101–200 +12%/wave, W201–300 +14%/wave, W301–400 +16%/wave, and so on indefinitely)
  • Ground armor climbs +2 every 100 waves past W100, capped at 30 (90% damage reduction) from W501 onwards; flying armor stays at 20
  • Ground stun resistance adds a second stage: W301–330 ramps from 30% to 60%, then caps
  • Flying stun resistance adds a second stage: W304–360 ramps from 30% to 60%, then caps
  • Wave 40 and 44 flying enemies softened (30k → 25k at W40; 36k → 32k at W44) to smooth the post-table HP spike
  • Yellow Sapphire chain buffed: +15% base damage (99–100 → 114–115) and +10% attack speed (1.0 → 1.1); Star Yellow Sapphire gains an extra hit per pass
  • Splash damage scaling uncapped past 100% of primary hit for Silver chain, Yellow Sapphire chain, and Ancient Blood Stone — more late-game scaling room
  • Great Opal wave tracked and shown in the leaderboard as a new "GOpal" column (the wave the first Great Opal is built)
  • Rocks (from discarded/combined gems) now persist in saves — maze pathing is fully intact after load
  • Leaderboard deduplicates repeated submissions from the same game, keeping only the highest-wave result
  • Mobile: pinch-to-zoom up to 4x and one-finger pan while zoomed; double-tap resets zoom

v1.9 — Save/Load & Mobile

  • Manual save/load: 3 save slots accessible via the Save/Load button; saves capture full game state during build phase
  • Mobile support: touch input for phones and tablets — tap to place/select, drag to preview placement
  • Responsive layout: on narrow screens the left panel hides and the info panel stacks below the canvas

v1.8 — Balance & Scaling

  • Crit rebalance: Diamond chance standardized to 25% across all qualities; crit multiplier scaling halved to +0.05/level
  • Great Pink Diamond crit mult reduced 10→8; Ancient Blood Stone 4→3; Gold chain chance lowered
  • Splash/nova damage capped at 100% of primary hit
  • Burn aura scaling changed from flat +2/level to +3%/level (Star Ruby, Uranium)
  • Attack speed per-level scaling removed from Aquamarine and Malachite chain
  • Opal attack speed aura buffed across all qualities (15/20/25/30/40/60%)
  • Great Sapphire range increased to 20 tiles; Great Emerald DoT nerfed to 32 dps / 100s
  • Ground damage resistance removed; replaced with speed scaling 6→8 t/s (waves 101–130)
  • Ground stun resistance simplified: +1%/wave from 101, caps at 30%
  • Flying enemies gain gradual speed ramp (caps ~wave 74); speed increases to 7 t/s from wave 200
  • Flying stun resistance added from wave 204 (+2% per flying wave, caps 30%)
  • Armor stays at 18 until wave 52, then 20
  • Stun immunity shown in enemy tooltip; Opal Attunement shown in gem chances panel
  • Gem quality names capitalized (Chipped, Flawed, etc.)
  • Comments now require a name; max length increased to 500 characters

v1.7 — Speed & Control

  • Game speed toggle (1x / 2x / 4x) — persists across waves
  • Per-gem attack toggle — disable gems to funnel kills; resets each wave
  • Enemy path now renders above fixed lane corridors for clear visibility

v1.6 — Visual Overhaul

  • Opal gems shifted to pearly white with iridescent shimmer — distinct from Topaz and Diamond
  • Aquamarine shifted to turquoise — distinct from Emerald
  • Flawed quality now renders as triangle; Chipped is chunkier with crack detail
  • All non-chipped gems have a specular highlight for polished look
  • Special gem upgrade tiers show escalating glow, accent rings, and brighter icons
  • Enemy path now olive-gold with dotted overlay — clearly distinct from brown lanes
  • Grass, rocks, lanes, and gem sockets all have more texture and depth
  • Ground and flying enemy sprites redesigned with more detail
  • Projectiles now tapered tracers with impact dots
  • Crit numbers have outlines and scale animation
  • HP bars wider with green → yellow → red gradient
  • Stun rings pulse; aura rings use double-ring pattern
  • Enemy info panel now shows distance traveled in tiles
  • Gem damage breakdown (Direct, Splash, DoT, Aura) shown when a gem has multiple damage sources

v1.5 — Enemy Resistance

  • Ground enemies gain damage and stun resistance starting at wave 100
  • Resistance scales linearly: +0.5% damage / +1% stun per wave, capping at wave 200 (50% dmg / 100% stun)
  • Resistance degrades as enemies travel through the maze — 800 tiles of maze fully removes it
  • Long mazes are now essential for high-wave survival; short stun-tower mazes are countered
  • Flying enemies are unaffected by resistance
  • Resistance shown in enemy info panel when selecting an enemy

v1.4 — Visual Overhaul

  • Fixed corridors between checkpoints now appear as brown muddy road
  • Empty buildable tiles now appear as dark green grass
  • Rocks alternate between two shades for visual separation
  • All gems now render as pre-cached sprites with gradient fills and detailed shapes
  • Special gems have unique silhouettes, color gradients, and inner icons — each chain is visually distinct
  • Enemies redesigned: ground units as armored figures, flying units as wing shapes
  • Board tiles refreshed with subtle gradients, beveled rocks, and softer grid lines
  • Flying path dashed line removed — checkpoint arrows indicate the route
  • Performance improved: sprite caching replaces per-frame primitive drawing

v1.3 — Opal Attunement

  • Opal Attunement: after reaching max gem level, each round increases the chance of rolling a second Perfect Opal when one is already placed (+0.1%/round, cap 10%). Resets permanently once a Great Opal is created
  • Aura range is now more generous — triggers at ~25% gem coverage instead of 50%
  • Splash scaling: +1px range and +1% damage per gem level for all splash gems
  • Crit chance scaling: +0.1% per gem level for all crit gems
  • New HUD metric: Leaks (total lives lost)
  • Leaderboard now tracks Leaks and MVP Gem (highest-kill gem)
  • Fix: downgraded gems now get their name reset to match new quality
  • Fix: upgraded special gems now use numbered names to avoid duplicates
  • Timestamps added to leaderboard and comments

v1.2 — Attack Types

  • Every gem now has an attack type matching its gem family
  • Enemies have a rotating weakness — ground enemies cycle through all 8 types each wave
  • Flying enemies are always weak to Amethyst
  • Matching weakness: +75% damage. Non-matching: −10% damage
  • Attack type shown in gem info, weakness shown when selecting enemies
  • Left panel with always-visible game stats and top gem trackers
  • Emeralds now use greedy targeting — prefer enemies without poison or with weaker poison
  • Great Emerald buffed: 40 dps, 5-minute poison duration
  • Great Aquamarine buffed: 3.0 attacks/sec, +1 tile range
  • Rose Quartz Crystal gains +0.5 tiles range per level (attack and aura)
  • Lucky Asian Jade gold chance now scales with level

v1.1 — Social Patch

  • Leaderboard: submit your score and compete globally
  • Comments: leave messages for other players
  • Patch Notes tab added
  • Forfeit button: end a game early and log your score
  • New HUD metrics: defend time and maze length
  • Win condition: fill the entire board with gems to win
  • Scores track game version and result (Win/Lose/Forfeit)

v1.0 — Launch

  • Great gems: 6th quality tier, obtained by combining 2 Perfect or 4 Flawless gems
  • 4-Combine: merge 4 gems of the same type and quality to skip a tier
  • Downgrade: reduce a kept gem one quality tier during the defend phase
  • Repick: spend gold to reroll all 5 placed gems during build phase
  • Endless waves: game continues past wave 36 with scaling enemies
  • Buy Extra Life: spend gold for more lives (max 20)
  • 13 special gem chains with level-based effect scaling
  • Slow capped at 50%, splash ranges fixed, crit chance no longer scales
  • Level bonus and MVP bonus are now additive
